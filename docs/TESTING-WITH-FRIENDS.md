# Testing subcult with friends

subcult is a daily magazine for your subculture: every day you get a new numbered issue (No. 001, 002, …) with the tea, fits, grails, sounds and what's rising in your scenes. It's a test version, so things will break. Telling us how is the whole point.

Use your phone if you can. That's what it's built for.

## Getting in (5 minutes)

1. Open the link we sent you. If it came as `/join/<handle>`, you're one of the first 50. You'll get a numbered "Founding 50" badge once you pick a handle.
2. Tap **Get your issue** and sign in with Google or an email link. Or **Continue as guest**: everything works except posting, friends and alerts.
3. Pick a handle. It's how friends find you; your email is never shown.
4. Take the **vibe check**. It takes 10 to 16 swipes and adapts as you go: right means "that's me", left means "not me". On "this or that" cards, tap the one you'd wear.
5. Keep, swap or add niches, and your first issue opens.

On iPhone, add it to your home screen (Share → Add to Home Screen) so push alerts work.

## What to try

- **Read today's issue top to bottom.** Does the cover make you want to scroll? Does it feel like a magazine, or like a music app?
- **Cop or Drop** a few grails, fits, tracks and pins. You'll see how everyone else voted. It rates items, never people.
- **Open a story** (the circles at the top). There's at most two music frames, and there's a "this or that" poll.
- **Open a niche page.** Each niche has its own issue.
- **Shop the look** on a fit, and **Alert me for similar** on an archive listing. Then check Me → Alerts.
- **Post your first fit** (Me → Post your fit).
- **Invite a friend** from Me → Friends. Once they've signed up, compare your vibe match.
- **Retake the vibe check** in a few days. It should show you different cards and tell you whether your era shifted.

## Things we know about

- Some niches are still thin, especially for grails, moodboard pins and fits. That's a supply problem we're fixing, not a bug.
- Music previews are 30 seconds; full songs open in Spotify or Apple Music.
- Push alerts need the home-screen app on iPhone.

## How to report something

Send us a screenshot and tell us:

- what you tapped, what you expected, and what happened instead
- your phone and browser (for example "iPhone 15, Safari")
- the issue number and date at the top of the cover, if it's about Today

Gut reactions help too: anything that felt boring, confusing, cringe or "this is just Spotify". Nothing is too small.

## Privacy

Your taste profile is built from what you tap, swipe and play. Your profile can be public or friends-only (Me → Friends). Listening status is off unless you turn it on. Sign out from Me.
