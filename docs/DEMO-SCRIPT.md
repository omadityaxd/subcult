# Demo GIF: what to record

A 45-second screen recording for the top of the README. Save it as `docs/demo.gif`, under 10 MB, and replace the
placeholder comment in the README with `![subcult demo](docs/demo.gif)`.

## Setup

- Production site, logged out, fresh browser profile (no saved taste), dark mode.
- Chrome DevTools device toolbar at **iPhone 14 Pro (393×852)**, zoom 100%. Hide the cursor or use
  a touch-style one.
- Sound off in the GIF, but let a preview play: the player UI moving is what sells it.
- Record at 30 fps with a screen recorder (ScreenToGif, Kap or OBS) and export at about 600px wide, 15 fps.

## Tips

- Do one silent rehearsal so caches are warm and nothing shows a loading state on camera.
- If a source is quiet that day, pick another featured niche rather than editing frames.
- No real people's private profiles in frame; use your own account or a guest.
- Keep it honest: don't stage content or fake numbers in the recording.
